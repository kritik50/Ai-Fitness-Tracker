"Fitness Tracker is a modern, AI-powered wellness application designed to reduce the friction of daily health monitoring by heavily leveraging Google's Gemini AI. While the foundation is a robust React and Strapi stack, the core value proposition lies in its intelligent automation: instead of manually searching for foods and estimating calories, users simply upload an image, and our backend utilizes Gemini 2.5 Flash vision capabilities to instantly extract the exact food item and nutritional data via structured JSON generation. Furthermore, the application features an AI Health Coach that dynamically aggregates 7 days of the user's dietary and activity logs, compares them against specific caloric targets, and utilizes targeted prompt engineering to deliver highly personalized, data-driven actionable insights. By shifting the heavy lifting of data analysis to AI, the project transforms a standard tracking tool into a proactive, intelligent personal health companion."
